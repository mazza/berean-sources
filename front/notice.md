# About this text

This repository is not an official publication of Berean Bible or Bible Hub.
Berean dedicated their texts to the public domain (CC0) on 30 April 2023 so
that others could use them. The repository is named `berean-sources` because
these files are drawn from more than one of their downloads. It is not the
Berean Standard Bible, the Berean Literal Bible, the Berean Study Bible, the
Berean Greek Bible, or the Berean Interlinear Bible.

The Hebrew and Aramaic Old Testament is the text Berean distributes in their
tables, in the Westminster Leningrad Codex tradition, with English verse labels.
The Greek New Testament is the Berean Greek Bible, whose base text is Nestle 1904.
Verses widely printed in the Byzantine and Textus Receptus traditions, and absent
from that critical base, are included in the chapter text for completeness.
Each of those verses keeps a footnote that says where it is attested and that
many early manuscripts lack it. The list is `pipeline/critical_verses.tsv`.

Berean ask that works which vary from their official text not use the Berean
name. This text varies in that one way. The name here only identifies the CC0
source, and the pages above say that this is not their official text.
